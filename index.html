About AG Ikenebgu

Growing Together in Faith, Hope, and Love

We are a spirit-filled Assemblies of God family committed to preaching the full Gospel, nurturing believers, and reaching our community with the love of Jesus Christ. Whether you are new to faith or returning home, you belong here.

Our Vision

A thriving church where every generation encounters God and lives transformed by the Gospel.

Our Mission

To worship God, disciple believers, strengthen families, and serve Ikenebgu with compassion and hope.

"Together we are building lives, families, and our community on the foundation of Christ."
