Our Legacy

SDTG Through The Years

Six editions. Six themes. One unchanging mission — to send down His glory.

SDTG 2020

Faith In The Fire

Virtual and in-person hybrid edition reaching 80K viewers. First global prayer chain with 120 nations participating.

SDTG 2021

Digital Reach

Pandemic-era virtual crusade with 200K live viewers. Launch of SDTG global prayer network.

SDTG 2022

Return Of Glory

Post-pandemic comeback with doubled attendance. SDTG Youth Movement and choir academy launched.

SDTG 2023

Revival Fire

First international livestream — 500K+ viewers. Delegates from 32 nations in attendance.

SDTG 2024

Heaven's Sound

Three nights of uninterrupted worship. Documented wheelchair deliverances and notable healings.

SDTG 2025

Glory Without Limits

30,000 attendees. 5,000+ souls saved. Featured on global Christian networks worldwide.
