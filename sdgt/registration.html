Traveling From Abroad?

International Attendees

We've prepared resources to make your journey to Owerri seamless and blessed.

Accommodation

Partner hotels offering discounted rates for SDTG attendees. Indicate your accommodation need during registration and our hospitality team will contact you with options.

Transportation

Free shuttle services from Sam Mbakwe International Airport (Owerri) and designated pickup points in Port Harcourt and Enugu on crusade days.

Travel Recommendations

Fly into Owerri (QOW) or Port Harcourt (PHC). Arrive at least one day early. Bring valid ID and check visa requirements for Nigeria.

International Desk

Contact our international team at intl@sdtg.org or WhatsApp +234 803 456 7890 for personalized travel assistance.
