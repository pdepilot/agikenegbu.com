Our Story

Where It All
Began

A small prayer meeting. A burning desire. A God who answers.

2014 Owerri,
Nigeria

In the heart of Owerri, a small gathering of 800 believers came together with a single prayer: "Lord, send down Your glory." What began as an intimate worship night would ignite a movement that would span continents and generations.

The founding vision was simple yet profound — to create a space where the presence of God was not just invited but expected; where every prayer ascended with the faith of thousands, and every worship song became a prophetic declaration over nations.

"We did not build an event. We built an altar — and God has been faithful to meet us there every single year, with signs and wonders that no human hand could orchestrate."

Year after year, the crusade grew — not through marketing budgets or celebrity endorsements, but through the undeniable testimony of changed lives. By 2019, delegates were flying in from 15 nations. By 2023, the livestream reached over half a million viewers worldwide. Today, SDTG stands as one of Africa's most celebrated international gospel crusades, carrying the fire of revival from Owerri to the ends of the earth.

The People Behind The Glory

Leadership

Rev. Bethel Nwanebu

Founder & Visionary
The Founder

A Vision Born
In The Secret Place

Rev. Bethel Nwanebu received the mandate for Send Down Thy Glory during an extended season of prayer and fasting in 2013. What God showed him was not merely a crusade — it was a portal through which heaven would pour revival upon a generation.

With a background spanning pastoral ministry, music, and intercessory prayer, he carries a rare combination of gifts that make SDTG uniquely positioned to touch every dimension of the human spirit — intellect, emotion, and the deepest longings of the soul.

Under his leadership, the crusade has grown from a local gathering to an internationally recognized move of God, receiving features on global Christian networks and drawing delegates from six continents.

The Worship Director

Crafting Atmospheres
Of Encounter

Minister Adaeze Ikenebgu leads the worship dimension of SDTG with an anointing that consistently ushers thousands into profound encounters with God. Her prophetic sensitivity and musical excellence set the tone for every session of the crusade.

What We Believe

Our Core Values

Spirit-Led Worship

We believe genuine worship cannot be manufactured or choreographed. Every service is yielded to the Holy Spirit's direction, creating spontaneous, authentic encounters with God's presence.

Word-Rooted Ministry

All we do is anchored in Scripture. Messages are prepared in prayer, delivered with clarity, and measured against the eternal truth of God's Word — never compromised for crowd approval.

Kingdom Unity

SDTG is a gathering of the whole body of Christ — across denominations, tribes, and nations. We celebrate diversity as a reflection of the Kingdom's breadth and beauty.

Expectant Faith

We come expecting God to show up with signs, wonders, and miracles. Our meetings are not religious formalities — they are divine appointments where the impossible becomes testimony.

Generational Investment

We are deeply committed to raising the next generation of worshippers and ministers. The SDTG Youth Movement and Choir Academy exist to pass the revival torch forward.

Compassionate Outreach

Glory is not confined to the crusade grounds. Our charity initiatives bring the tangible love of Christ to the marginalised, demonstrating that revival transforms whole communities.
